## StradaOS 2.17.0 — Navigation with HERE and traffic, English and Arabic

### New
- **Turn-by-turn navigation inside StradaOS**: search a place, see it on the map first, then tap **Go**. Back returns to the results.
- **HERE search and routes** (optional, with your HERE API key in *Settings → Map & weather → Navigation*): better search and routes that know the current traffic. Without a key, OpenStreetMap is used as before.
- **Traffic on your route**: slow stretches are orange and very slow ones red, and the bottom bar shows the delay (for example "+6 min traffic"). It refreshes every 5 minutes while you drive.
- **HERE also works on Android 4.x screens**, thanks to a modern encryption library used only for HERE.
- **Built-in music player** for songs on USB, SD card or internal storage.
- **Radio mode in the home player** (in testing): on XY/PVE screens (AC8317) StradaOS runs the radio itself, without opening the radio app: ▶ / ■ turn it on and off, previous/next go to your previous/next saved station (with none saved, they search), the station shows in the player, and tapping the player or the Radio button opens a **radio screen**: ‹ › tune 0.1 MHz at a time (9 kHz on AM) so you can find stations by hand, and pressing and holding them searches automatically; **Frequency** lets you type one (for example 101.1); big buttons for your saved stations (tap to listen, press and hold to rename or remove); FM / AM; and **Save**, which lets you give the station a name (the radio doesn't send station names). If the radio doesn't answer a search within 10 seconds, it stops searching and goes to your next saved station. On other screens the buttons control the factory radio app.
- **Send diagnostics to your phone over Wi-Fi**: *Settings → System → Send diagnostics to phone* shows a QR code; scan it and the file downloads (iPhone too).
- **Theme by schedule or by the system**, and Wi-Fi / Bluetooth icons at the top.

### Changed
- The app is now in **English and Arabic**, and **Arabic is right-to-left**.
- Diagnostics now include the player status, the last 200 events (kept even after StradaOS restarts) and the crash log, and say so when they can't be saved.
- Test builds show a build number (for example 2.17.0-prueba.152-eaac007), so every test build can be told apart.
